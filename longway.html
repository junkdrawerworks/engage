The long way home

Stranded in the Delta Quadrant, 70,000 light years from Earth, as Voyager was. Each turn is a quarter of a year: choose how fast to fly and what to stop for. Your collectors scoop up enough fuel to keep you at warp 7.8 for ever. Faster burns it by the square of the speed.

The Milky Way from above, to scale: 100,000 light years across, the Sun about 26,000 from the centre.

Cruise on flies quarter after quarter until something needs you: somewhere to stop, the tanks running dry, the stores getting low, or home.

Home

    Speeds are Engage’s own warp scale, the one the bridge flies by: warp 9.975, Voyager’s top cruising speed, is about 6,700 times the speed of light. The 70,000 light years and the 75-year journey are the show’s, from its first episode, “Caretaker”. Voyager came home in seven years by shortcuts the writers invented; this trip has none. Its fuel, stores and stops are a game’s, not canon.
