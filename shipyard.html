    None kept yet. Each one you keep is listed here, with a link to race her on the warp scale.

      The hulls are this game’s own types. Each is said to be like a real Starfleet class for scale, from Memory Alpha’s pages on the Oberth, Defiant, Nova, Constitution, Intrepid and Galaxy classes. How a hull’s mass turns into speed, crew, sensors and defence is the game’s. Cruising warp is a third of her top speed, on the warp scale’s own curve, and no design goes past warp 9.975, Voyager’s top cruising speed. Starfleet designed and built its ships at the Utopia Planitia Fleet Yards, in drafting rooms on Mars and drydocks in orbit round it (Memory Alpha, “Utopia Planitia Fleet Yards”).
