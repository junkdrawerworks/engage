Distress call

A signal on the emergency band

Survivors on a crippled ship can’t say where they are. All they can tell you is what their sky looks like: the colour of their sun, where our Sun sits among their stars and how faint it looks. Every star has a sky of its own. Find theirs on Earth’s real sky, lock on, and a rescue ship goes. Their air is running out as you look.

How to read their sky
  • Where our Sun sits. If they see our Sun in Hercules, they are on the opposite side of our sky from Hercules. Turn round, under the chart, takes you there.
  • How faint our Sun looks. The fainter it is, the farther they are: magnitude 2 from about 9 light years, 4 from about 22, 6 (as faint as the eye can see) from about 56.
  • Their sun’s colour. Tap a star on the chart for its colour and how far it is.
  • Cleaning up the signal gives another clue and costs air. So does locking on to the wrong star.
