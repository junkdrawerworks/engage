The Prime Directive

General Order 1: no interference with a people who haven’t reached warp, not even to let them know you exist. A board of inquiry gives you a case where it and the right thing pull apart. Give your orders, and the board grades your compassion, restraint, judgment and candour, and tells you what a captain on screen did. Nobody earns all four.
