Astrometrics

Every star Engage knows, on one wall: hot on the left, cool on the right, bright at the top, dim at the bottom. Astronomers call it the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram, and on it stars fall into families.

Tap a star on the wall for its card.

Stars: the HYG catalogue; temperatures and true brightness are worked out from each star’s colour and distance, or measured, for the stars with notes. The main sequence: Pecaut and Mamajek 2013, Astrophysical Journal Supplement 208, 9, as kept up to date by Mamajek. The Sun’s life: Sackmann, Boothroyd and Kraemer 1993, Astrophysical Journal 418, 457.
